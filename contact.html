Frequently Asked Questions

Standard structural, electrical, and life-safety guidelines for commercial and private installations.

Standard residential modular lifts take approximately 4 to 6 weeks for precision factory fabrication and 3 to 5 business days for mechanical and electrical installation in a prepared shaft.

Heavy-duty commercial traction setups with 10+ floors typically require 8 to 12 weeks for custom manufacturing and 2 to 4 weeks for on-site rail alignment, cab assembly, and city inspection certifications.

Before our engineers arrive for rail placement, the following civil prerequisites must be completed:

  • Plumb and square vertical hoistway with certified load-bearing lintels at every landing.
  • Waterproofed hoistway pit (minimum 200mm for home lifts, 1100mm+ for commercial traction).
  • Dedicated 3-phase 415V (or single-phase 230V for small residential units) electrical supply with an isolator switch at the uppermost level.
  • Ventilation louvers or mechanical shaft exhaust per local fire and safety authority codes.

No. 100% of our residential home lift catalog is Machine-Room-Less (MRL). The compact, high-efficiency gearless drive is integrated inside the headroom of the hoistway, and the slim microprocessor control cabinet is mounted flush with the wall next to the top landing door, eliminating the need for a rooftop penthouse or equipment room.

Every Nima elevator features a fail-safe Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) paired with a smart lithium backup array.

In the event of a power cut, the ARD instantly engages within 1.2 seconds, automatically glides the cabin smoothly to the nearest adjacent floor utilizing gravity-favored descent, releases the electromechanical brakes, unlocks the car doors, and illuminates cabin emergency lighting. Passengers simply step out safely without manual external intervention.
