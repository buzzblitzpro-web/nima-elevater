Silent Strength, Built for Kerala's Climate.

Founded in 1998 in Kochi by mechanical structuralists and electrical drive pioneers, Nima Elevator was established to solve Kerala's unique architectural challenges: high coastal salinity, monsoon humidity, elevated water tables, and domestic power grid requirements.

For over 27 years, from prestigious waterfront homes in Panampilly Nagar to IT commercial towers at Infopark Kakkanad and medical centers in Kozhikode, our engineering teams have rejected off-the-shelf compromises. We design, calibrate, and program our own direct-drive permanent magnet synchronous traction motors (PMSM) and 100% Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) fail-safe systems.

Our residential home lifts operate seamlessly on standard 230V single-phase KSEB domestic power or solar backup, requiring zero overhead machine rooms and low-pit depths (150-200mm) that eliminate water ingress risks in Kerala's coastal terrain.

shield The Zero-Harm Safety Covenant (BIS IS 14665 Compliant)

Every Nima lift installed in Kerala is certified under BIS IS 14665 & IS 15259 with full clearance from the Kerala Electrical Inspectorate. Equipped with triple-redundant mechanical governor catches, bidirectional seismic damping clamps, and optical door sensors tested over 10,000,000 duty cycles.
